## refund
Refunds are available for delivered orders within 30 days of delivery. Refunds above 2000 require human approval.

## address_change
Shipping address can only be changed while the order is still processing (not yet shipped).

## order_status
Order status can be shared with the verified customer who placed the order.

## other
Anything outside refunds, address changes, and order status goes to a human agent.
